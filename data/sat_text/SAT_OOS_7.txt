QUESTION:
If y = x^3 + 2x + 5 and z = x^2 + 7x + 1, what is 2y + z in terms of x?

CHOICES:
A) 3x^3 + 11x + 11
B) 2x^3 + x^2 + 9x + 6
C) 2x^3 + x^2 + 11x + 11
D) 2x^3 + 2x^2 + 18x + 12

ANSWER: C

EXPLANATION: 2y = 2x^3 + 4x + 10; adding z gives 2x^3 + x^2 + 11x + 11.
