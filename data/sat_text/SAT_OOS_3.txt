QUESTION:
If 2/(a - 1) = m/y, and y != 0 where a != 1, what is y in terms of a? (m is a randomized value: 4, 8, 12, 16 or 20; the page stores it in localStorage as 'midVariable'. There is no image.)

CHOICES:
A) (m/2)a - m/2  (page option A, shown as 'ans' a - 'ans' where ans = m/2)
B) 3-5a
C) 2-4a
D) 4-2a
E) 6-3a (page option E)

ANSWER: A

EXPLANATION: Cross-multiplying gives 2y = m(a - 1), so y = (m/2)(a - 1) = (m/2)a - m/2. Options B-E are fixed distractors.
