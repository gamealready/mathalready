QUESTION:
In the complex number system, which of the following is equal to (14 - 2i)(7 + 12i)? (Note: i = sqrt(-1))

CHOICES:
A) 74
B) 122
C) 74 + 154i
D) 122 + 154i

ANSWER: D

EXPLANATION: 98 + 168i - 14i - 24i^2 = 98 + 24 + 154i = 122 + 154i.
