QUESTION:
Line l is graphed in the xy-plane. [Diagram: a straight line l sloping downward from upper left to lower right, crossing the y-axis at about y = 6 and the x-axis at about x = 4, with marked points at (0,6), (2,3), (4,0) and further points down to the right; axes labeled -5 and 5.] If line l is translated up 5 units and right 7 units, what is the slope of the new line?

CHOICES:
A) 2/5
B) -3/2
C) -8/9
D) -11/14

ANSWER: B

EXPLANATION: A translation does not change slope. Slope = (3-6)/(2-0) = -3/2.
