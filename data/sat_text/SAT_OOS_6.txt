QUESTION:
If a^(-1/2) = x, where a > 0 and x > 0, which of the following equations gives a in terms of x?

CHOICES:
A) a = 1/sqrt(x)
B) a = 1/x^2
C) a = sqrt(x)
D) a = -x^2

ANSWER: B

EXPLANATION: a^(-1/2) = x, so a = x^(-2) = 1/x^2.
