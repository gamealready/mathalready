About Us

This is a website which can help students and users when learning new formula. I have seen there are a lot of on-line calculators. Some of them only show algorithms or derivations; some of them put source code or excel files on their website; some of them have interface but with limited formula. However none of them offer simple calculators with a decent interface. My goal is to have calculators for those formula which already are well-known, so users and students can easily understand the apply the formula.
