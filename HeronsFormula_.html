Heron's Formula

photo by Jamgoodman

In geometry, Heron's formula (or Hero's formula) gives the area of a triangle in terms of the three side lengths. If s = 1 2 ( a + b + c ) {\textstyle s={\tfrac {1}{2}}(a+b+c)} is the semiperimeter of the triangle, the area is,

A = s ( s − a ) ( s − b ) ( s − c ) . {\displaystyle A={\sqrt {s(s-a)(s-b)(s-c)}}.}
